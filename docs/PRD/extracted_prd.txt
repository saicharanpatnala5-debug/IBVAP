[EXECUTIVE MISSION STATEMENT] 
This platform is designed to provide 24/7 autonomous border perimeter surveillance across rugged, unfenced Himalayan terrain, eliminating operator fatigue, suppressing wildlife false alarms by 85%, and delivering sub-15ms threat recognition with fail-safe offline edge resilience.
1. Executive Summary & Strategic Vision
The Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB) is entrusted with safeguarding India's open, unfenced borders with Nepal and Bhutan. Unlike heavily militarized borders equipped with physical concertina wire fences, floodlit corridors, and continuous patrolling roads, the Indo-Nepal (1,751 km) and Indo-Bhutan (699 km) borders feature open visa-free trans-border movement under bilateral treaties. This open-border paradigm introduces acute national security vulnerabilities: illegal infiltration, smuggling of narcotics, arms, and fake Indian currency (FICN), human trafficking, and cross-border criminal safe havens. Traditional CCTV surveillance across Forward Border Outposts (BOPs) fails because manual monitoring of dozens of high-definition video feeds causes severe operator cognitive fatigue within 20 minutes, leading to missed incursions and critical security lapses.
The Intelligent Border Video Analytics Platform (IBVAP) transforms conventional passive CCTV and thermal LWIR camera grids into an autonomous, proactive threat-detection shield. By marrying quantized Edge AI inference on low-power rugged hardware (NVIDIA Jetson) with centralized high-throughput event correlation and predictive multi-camera tracking, IBVAP delivers instant tactical situational awareness to BOP QRT units and Sector Command Headquarters.
2. Problem Formulation & Operational Challenges
The platform addresses four core operational bottlenecks identified in SSB field deployment reports:
Unfenced Open Border Topology: 
Vast stretches of border run through tea estates, dense Terai jungle, riverine gaps, and remote Himalayan ridgelines where building physical walls is geophysically impossible.
Adverse Environmental Conditions: 
Dense fog in the Himalayan foothills and midnight darkness blind standard optical sensors. Furthermore, cattle, wild elephants, and blowing foliage trigger rampant false alarms in primitive motion-detection systems.
Extreme Backhaul Bandwidth Limitations: 
Forward outposts rely on satellite VSAT links or terrestrial RF microwave links that frequently suffer severe packet drops or total blackout during monsoons and landslides.
Fragmented Intelligence Silos: 
Perimeter breaches do not occur in isolation. Infiltration syndicates orchestrate diversionary drop-offs, scout check-posts with unregistered vehicles, and exploit blind spots between non-communicating camera feeds.

5. Detailed Functional Specifications
5.1 Perception & Object Detection Subsystem (FR-01)
Multi-Class Border Perception: 
The system shall simultaneously detect Persons, Vehicles (Car, Truck, Motorcycle, Bus), and Wildlife/Livestock at minimum 25 FPS on 1080p video streams.
Adaptive Confidence: 
The model confidence threshold shall be dynamically tuned (0.35 night/thermal, 0.55 daytime optical) to optimize Recall without raising false positive alarms.
Suppression & De-duplication: 
All detections shall pass through Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS) with IoU threshold 0.45 to prevent duplicate track allocations.
5.2 Multi-Object Tracking & Trajectory Analysis (FR-02)
2-Stage Association: 
The tracker shall utilize a two-stage association ByteTrack algorithm powered by an 8-dimensional Kalman filter state vector [u, v, s, r, du, dv, ds, dr].
Trajectory Vectoring: 
The tracker shall compute instantaneous velocity, cumulative travel distance, and heading angle (0-360 degrees) to classify movement as INWARD (incursion toward India) or OUTWARD.
Track Continuity: 
Tracks shall persist across transient occlusions (up to 30 frames / 1.2 seconds) before retirement.
5.3 Virtual Polygonal Zones & Virtual Tripwires (FR-03)
Arbitrary Polygons: 
Commanders shall be able to define arbitrary n-sided polygonal security zones with normalized [0.0, 1.0] coordinates.
Security Tiering: 
Zones shall support three strict security tiers: RED_RESTRICTED (zero dwell tolerance, instant breach trigger), YELLOW_MONITORING (dwell threshold > 15s), and GREEN_NORMAL.
Microsecond Execution: 
Point-in-polygon containment shall be evaluated using a ray-casting intersection algorithm executing in under 2 microseconds per target.
5.4 ANPR & Vehicle Intelligence (FR-04)
High-Speed Capture: 
The system shall extract license plate text from high-speed approach vehicles at up to 60 km/h.
Syntax Enforcement: 
Plate text shall be validated against a standardized Indian State Prior dictionary (DL, PB, JK, HR, UP, BR, WB, AS, UK, etc.) and format rules (XX 00 XX 0000).
Majority Voting: 
A 5-frame rolling majority voting filter shall eliminate OCR character jitter before committing plates to the database.
5.5 Facial Recognition & Watchlist Compliance (FR-05)
Normalized Embeddings: 
The system shall compute 128-dimensional L2-normalized facial embeddings using lightweight YuNet and SFace architectures.
Similarity Threshold: 
Cosine similarity threshold of >= 0.65 shall trigger an alert against national intelligence watchlists.
Human-in-the-Loop Mandate: 
In strict compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, automated facial matches shall require manual operator confirmation before biometric logging.

7. Statutory DPDP Act 2023 & Security Architecture
IBVAP is architected from the ground up to comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and MHA security directives:
Data Minimization & Auto-Purging: 
Surveillance footage of civilian non-infringing crossings is purged after 30 days. Only footage tagged to confirmed security incidents is archived for evidentiary proceedings.
Data-at-Rest AES-256 Encryption: 
All offline buffer databases on forward edge nodes and all video clips in central storage are encrypted using AES-256-GCM. Physical theft of an edge device does not compromise surveillance intelligence.
Tamper-Evident SHA-256 Chained Audit Trail: 
Every system mutation (alert ack, zone modification, user login, video export) writes to an append-only audit log where each entry contains the SHA-256 hash of the previous record, preventing forensic tampering.
Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): 
Five discrete security roles (Admin, Supervisor, CCTV Operator, Field QRT, Auditor) enforce strict principle of least privilege.
